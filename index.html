Hi, I'm Sanskar Acharya, a BSc.CSIT student who enjoys learning, building, and exploring new technologies. My journey into programming started with curiosity, and over time it has grown into a genuine passion for creating useful and meaningful software.

I'm currently focused on Python and Django, while also working with HTML, CSS, MySQL, Git, and GitHub. I enjoy building web applications that not only work well but are also clean, simple, and easy to use. Every project I work on teaches me something new and helps me become a better developer.
